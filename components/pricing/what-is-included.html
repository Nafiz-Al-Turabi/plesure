What is included (and what is not)

Included

  • Discreet coordination by appointment
  • Clear confirmation before meeting
  • Professional communication and alignment
  • Respect for privacy and boundaries

Not included

  • Hotel rooms or private venues
  • Drinks, dinner, tickets, transport
  • Entry fees, reservations, or event access

Any additional costs related to the setting are covered by the client and are briefly aligned in advance — so the experience remains smooth and without surprises.
