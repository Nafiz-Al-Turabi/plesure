Appointments arranged with care

Pleasure does not confirm arrangements through informal chat conversations. Instead, we work with a Booking Request flow to keep everything clear and efficient.

In your request, you share:

  • city
  • date & time window
  • desired duration
  • setting (hotel or private)
  • 2–3 profile preferences (recommended)

Based on this, you receive a discreet confirmation with the applicable guideline for your request.

This approach avoids repeated questions and allows us to confirm availability faster and more accurately.
